We study the prevalence of religious rhetoric in more than 1.4 million speeches from the U.S. Congressional Record since 1995. We measure religious language with a keyword list built from auxiliary corpora (Sermon Central and COCA), and identify exact and approximate quotations of the Bible using a paraphrase detection pipeline based on MPNet verse embeddings. While members of both parties routinely mention God and occasionally reference scripture, Republicans have increasingly used religious signals over the last two decades.
