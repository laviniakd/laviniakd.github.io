We assemble a suite of psychometric instruments (693 questions across 39 instruments) and evaluate many open-source language models with an accompanying measurement strategy. Even minor prompt perturbations substantially reduce models' question-answering ability and consistency, suggesting that prompting alone is insufficient to reliably capture model "perceptions."
