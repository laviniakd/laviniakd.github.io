We introduce TIDEs (Transphobia Identification in Digital Environments), a dataset and model for detecting transphobic speech, intended to support content moderation tools built by and for trans people. We describe care-centered practices for labeling data with trans and nonbinary annotators, and fine-tune a DeBERTa classifier for identifying transphobic posts on social media.
