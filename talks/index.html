Talks

  • Promises of (Platform) Permanence: How People Die on Legacy.com with Kevin Zheng Society for Social Studies of Science (4S) · Seattle, WA · September 2025

    We scraped roughly 100,000 obituaries from Legacy.com, the largest obituary aggregator in the United States, and analyzed them using lexical methods and LLM-based occupation identification, interpreting the results through platform studies and archival theory.

  • NLP in Archival Infrastructure with Jesse Johnston and Dallas Card Archives as Data · 2025

    A review of uses of natural language processing in archives, and a discussion of the affordances of LLMs in historical narration, e.g. for archival description.

  • Religious Rhetoric in the U.S. Congressional Record with Dallas Card International Conference on Computational Social Science (IC2S2) · Philadelphia, PA · July 2024

    We used a lexical model and a logistic regression-based classifier to estimate the prevalence of religious speech in Congress, and identified partisan patterns in references to the Bible via a neural embedding-based retrieval method.
